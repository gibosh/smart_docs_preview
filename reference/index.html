Reference¶

Definitions of the terms used in SMART, and fixes for common problems.

Glossary¶

Term

Meaning

Alert

A notice that something needs attention: an exclusion zone breach, an SOS or an unplugged device. See Safety and Alerts.

Alert rule

Says which events raise alerts, on which days and times, and who is contacted.

Alert workflow

The record of one alert, from when it is raised until it is resolved.

Alert zone

A zone that decides who is contacted when an SOS is raised inside it.

Basemap

The background map: roads and places, or satellite imagery.

Baseline

The version of the schedule that progress is measured against.

Beacon

A Bluetooth beacon that helps locate devices where GPS is weak, such as indoors.

Breach

A resource entering an exclusion zone while a rule says it shouldn’t.

Callsign

The name a resource goes by while a device is assigned to it, such as “EX01”.

Contact

A person who receives alerts. Contacts don’t need a SMART login.

Crew

A named set of roles that work together, used to add resources to schedule tasks.

Data date

The date a schedule’s progress was last updated.

Device

The tracker fitted to a resource.

Exclusion zone

An area that some or all resources must stay out of. Shown as Restricted on the map.

First responder

A contact who receives SMS as well as email for a rule’s breaches and SOS alerts.

Geofence

The area where SMART records tracking data.

Heatmap

A colour overlay in Window mode that shows where resources spent the most time.

Historic mode

Shows the site as it was at the time under the timeline cursor.

Live mode

Shows resources where they are now.

Parent zone

A larger zone that contains another, used to organise the site into areas.

Project

One site or job, with its own map, resources, zones and data.

Resource

A person or piece of equipment that SMART tracks.

Role

What a resource is or does, such as “Excavator”. Roles form a tree.

Root role

The top of a branch of the role tree, such as plant or personnel.

SMART Breakdown Structure (SBS)

A schedule activity code that links tasks to workzones.

SOS

An emergency alert raised by pressing the SOS button on a device.

Structure

A 3D model of a building or object placed on the map.

Task

An activity from the project schedule.

Time phasing

A setting that shows a workzone only for part of the project.

Window mode

Shows every position recorded between the two ends of a time window.

Workzone

An area where planned work happens. Shown as Work on the map.

Zone type

A category of zone, such as “Stockpile” or “Laydown”.

Troubleshooting¶

I can’t see my project¶

  • Check you signed in with the email address you were invited with.

  • On Select Project, finished projects show as Not Active. They are still there; select one and Load it.

  • If the project isn’t listed at all, ask your project’s administrator to add you in Users.

A resource isn’t on the map or isn’t moving¶

  • Check the mode. In Historic or Window mode the map shows the past. Select Map Mode, then Live. Live mode only appears while the project is active.

  • Check the filters. If Clear map filters is showing, a filter is hiding some resources. Select it to show everyone.

  • Check the layer settings. In Map Layers, Show personnel may be off, Hide charging Personnel devices may be hiding devices on charge, or Resource roles may be limited to some roles.

  • Check the device. In Device Management, the device’s status shows its battery, when it last reported and whether it is inside the geofence. A device that is off, flat or outside the geofence doesn’t report positions.

  • Check the assignment. A device only shows as a resource while it is assigned. Free devices aren’t on the map.

Times look wrong¶

SMART shows times in the project’s time zone unless you choose Local under Time Zone in Display settings. If you are in a different time zone from the site, check which one is selected.

Nobody received an alert¶

  • In Alert Management, check that the zone’s dot is orange or red. Grey means no rule is assigned, and yellow means the rules have no contacts.

  • Check the rule is Enabled and that the time of the breach falls within one of its time windows and its active dates.

  • Check the person’s role isn’t in the rule’s Allowed Resource Roles (Excluded from Alerts).

  • Use Simulate exclusion breach or Simulate alert on the Zones screen to preview who would be contacted.

The map is slow¶

In Map Layers, set Symbols to Icons, set Terrain quality to Performance, and shorten or turn off Trails (min).
