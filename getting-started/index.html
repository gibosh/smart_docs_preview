Getting StartedUpdated 23 Nov 2026

Getting Started¶

SMART shows where people and equipment are on your project, and where they have been. Small tracking devices report each resource’s position. SMART puts those positions on a 3D map of the site, together with your work areas, exclusion zones and schedule. You can watch the site live, replay any moment in the past and see how the work compares with the plan.

You don’t need to be a planner or a surveyor to use SMART. If you can use an online map, you can find your way around.

Your first ten minutes¶

  1. Sign in and choose a project.

  2. Learn the main parts of the screen: the map, the navigation panel and the tables.

  3. Move around the map: pan, zoom, rotate and tilt.

  4. Replay a past shift with the Timeline.

  5. Set the display settings that suit you, such as light or dark mode and your time zone.

Key terms¶

These words appear throughout SMART and this guide.

Term

What it means

Project

One construction site or job. Each project has its own map, resources, zones and data.

Resource

A person or piece of equipment that SMART tracks, such as an excavator, a truck or a site worker.

Device

The tracker fitted to a resource. It reports the resource’s position.

Role

What a resource is or does, for example Excavator or Traffic Controller. Roles are grouped in a hierarchy, and the top of each group is its root role.

Crew

A group of resources that work together.

Workzone

An area of the site where planned work happens.

Exclusion zone

An area that some or all resources must stay out of. Entering one is a breach.

Alert

A notice that something needs attention, such as a breach or an SOS from a device.

Task

An activity from your project schedule.

Structure

A 3D model of a building or structure placed on the map.

The Glossary has the full list.

Signing in and choosing a project¶

Signing in¶

  1. Go to www.smartcoro.com.

  2. Enter your Email and Password, then select Log in.

If you’ve forgotten your password, select Forgot password? on the sign-in page and follow the email instructions.

Screenshot

SMART sign-in page

Your administrator sets up your account. If you can’t sign in, or you don’t have an account, contact your project’s SMART administrator.

Choosing a project¶

After you sign in, the Select Project list shows every project you have access to.

Screenshot

Select Project list with one project selected

Each project is labelled:

  • Active: devices on site are sending data now. You can watch the site live.

  • Not Active: the project has finished or is paused. You can still replay past activity and view all analytics.

To open a project:

  1. Select the project in the list.

  2. Select Load.

The project opens on the map. If a project you expect is missing, select Refresh. If it’s still missing, ask your administrator for access.

Switching projects¶

  1. Select the SMART logo at the top left of the map to open the Main Menu.

  2. Under Project, select Project List.

  3. Choose another project and select Load.

Signing out¶

Open the Main Menu and select Logout.

Finding your way around¶

When you open a project, SMART shows the map at the top of the screen and a table area underneath.

Screenshot

The SMART screen with each area numbered: navigation panel, timeline, map buttons, map, table area, summary strip

The navigation panel¶

The navigation panel sits at the top left of the map. Use it to move between the main areas of SMART.

Button

What it opens

SMART logo

The Main Menu (see below)

Analytics

Dashboards for the project. See Analytics.

Resources

The table of tracked people and equipment. See Resources and Crews.

Zones

The table of workzones, exclusion zones and alert zones. See Zones and Structures.

Structures

The table of 3D structures on the map.

Tasks

The project schedule as a Gantt chart. See Tasks and Schedule.

Alerts

Alert Management (rules, zones and contacts) or Alert Workflows (alerts as they happen). See Safety and Alerts.

The button for the area you have open is highlighted.

The timeline¶

The timeline runs along the top of the map. It shows the time you are looking at and lets you replay the site. See The Timeline.

The map buttons¶

Buttons on the right of the map change what the map shows:

  • Map Mode: switch between Live, Historic and Window views. See Map modes.

  • Map Layers: choose what is drawn on the map. See Map layers.

  • Map Tools: drawing and measuring tools. See Map Tools.

  • Legend: explains the colours and symbols on the map.

  • Clear map filters: appears when a filter is on. Select it to show everything again. See Filters and search.

The scale bar and the current zoom level are at the bottom right of the map.

The table area¶

The table for the area you chose in the navigation panel appears under the map.

  • Drag the divider between the map and the tables to give either one more room.

  • Select the button on the divider to collapse or expand the tables.

The summary strip¶

Next to the tables, a summary strip shows the project at a glance:

  • Active: how many resources are reporting.

  • Breaches: exclusion zone breaches.

  • SOS: SOS alerts raised from devices.

  • A time period selector: 24h, Week, Month, All or Custom.

  • Weather: current conditions on site. Select it to open the weather observations.

The Resources & Crews panel¶

When you assign resources to tasks, a Resources & Crews panel opens on the left. It lists resources and crews you can search and select. See Crews.

The Main Menu¶

Select the SMART logo in the navigation panel to open the Main Menu. You only see the options your permissions allow.

Section

Options

Users

User Groups, Users (administrators)

Project

Device Management and Project Settings (administrators), Project List

Account

Display Settings, Recommendations (administrators), Videos, Help, Logout

Help opens this guide.

The footer¶

The bar at the bottom of the screen has a light/dark switch and links to the Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy.

Moving around the map¶

The SMART map is 3D. You can move across it, zoom in and out, turn it and tilt it to look across the site from a low angle.

Mouse controls¶

To

Do this

Move the map (pan)

Hold the left mouse button and drag.

Zoom in or out

Scroll the mouse wheel.

Turn or tilt the map

Hold the right mouse button and drag. Drag left or right to turn. Drag up or down to tilt.

Face north again

Select the compass.

Screenshot

The map tilted to show the site in 3D, with the compass highlighted

Going to an exact zoom level¶

The Zoom box at the bottom right of the map shows the current zoom level. Type a number in the box to jump to that level. Higher numbers are closer to the ground.

Flight mode¶

Flight mode lets you fly over the site as if you were in a drone.

  1. Select Enable flight mode in the timeline buttons.

  2. Use the keyboard to fly:

    • W: forward

    • S: back

    • A: left

    • D: right

  3. Move the mouse to look around.

  4. Press Escape or select Exit flight mode to return to the normal map.

Saved cameras¶

In flight mode you can save camera positions, for example a view of the main work front, and return to them later.

  1. Fly to the view you want.

  2. Select Map Layers on the right of the map. Under Saved cameras, select Save current flight camera.

  3. To return to a saved view, select Go to next to it.

You can rename, reorder and delete saved cameras, and show them on the map. Saved cameras are kept in your web browser for this project. They don’t move with you to another computer or browser.

Changing the background map¶

You can switch between a street-style map and satellite imagery, turn on 3D terrain and show elevation contours. See Map layers.

Display settings¶

Display settings change how SMART looks for you. They don’t affect anyone else, and they’re saved in your web browser.

To open them, select the SMART logo to open the Main Menu, then select Display Settings.

Screenshot

Display Settings

Setting

What it does

Light Mode / Dark Mode

Switches between a light and a dark screen. You can also use the switch in the footer at the bottom of the screen.

Tooltips

Shows a short label when you hover over a button.

Gantt Legend

Shows the legend on the Gantt chart in Tasks.

Font Size

Makes text smaller or larger.

Time Zone

Project shows times in the project’s local time. Local shows times in your computer’s time zone.

Reset to Default

Puts every display setting back to its original value.

Tip

If you work in a different time zone from the site, keep Time Zone set to Project. Times in SMART then match the times people on site use.
